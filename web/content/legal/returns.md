# Pawtella Returns & Refunds Policy

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026 · **Version:** 1.0

This Returns & Refunds Policy explains when and how you can return items bought from **Bone & Biscuit College West**, doing business as **Pawtella** (**"Pawtella," "we," "us"** or **"our"**), and how refunds work. It forms part of our Terms of Use (https://pawtella.com/terms) and should be read with our Shipping Policy (https://pawtella.com/shipping). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms.

> **The short version.** Unopened, unused items can be returned **within 30 days of delivery** with proof of purchase. For health and safety reasons, **opened food, treats and litter are not returnable** unless they are damaged, defective, recalled or the wrong item. If something arrives **damaged, defective, recalled or wrong**, tell us within **7 days** with photos and we will replace it or refund you at no cost to you. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within **5–10 business days** after we receive the return. **Paw Points you spent are restored on refunded orders.** Nothing here limits your rights under consumer protection law.

## Key Points at a Glance

This summary is for convenience only. The full Policy below controls.

- **Return window:** 30 days from delivery, for unopened and unused items, with proof of purchase.
- **Opened food, treats and litter:** not returnable, unless damaged, defective, recalled or the wrong item.
- **Damaged, defective, recalled or wrong items:** report within 7 days with photos for a free replacement or refund.
- **Refund method and timing:** original payment method, usually 5–10 business days after we receive the return.
- **Paw Points:** points spent are restored on refunded orders; purchased Paw Point packs are non-refundable once points are used.
- **Return shipping:** paid by you, unless the return is due to our error.

---

## 1. Return Window and Condition

1.1 **30 days.** You may return eligible items **within 30 days of delivery** (or of pickup, for pickup orders).

1.2 **Condition.** To be eligible, items must be **unopened and unused**, in their original condition and packaging (including tags, seals and any manufacturer packaging), and resalable.

1.3 **Proof of purchase.** You must provide **proof of purchase**, such as your order number or confirmation email.

## 2. Items That Are Not Returnable

For the health and safety of pets, and for hygiene reasons, the following are **not returnable once opened or used**, **except** where they are damaged, defective, recalled or the wrong item (Section 3), or where the law requires otherwise:

- **opened or used pet food, treats, supplements and chews;**
- **opened litter;**
- opened health, hygiene or grooming products;
- items marked **final sale** or clearance at the time of purchase;
- gift cards and **Paw Point packs** (see Section 6);
- items that have been used, washed, damaged by the customer, or are missing parts or packaging, and perishable goods.

If you are unsure whether an item is returnable, contact us at **support@pawtella.com** before opening it.

## 3. Damaged, Defective, Recalled or Wrong Items

3.1 **We will make it right.** If an item arrives **damaged or defective**, is subject to a **safety recall**, or is **not the item you ordered**, you are entitled to a **free replacement or refund**, including the cost of return shipping where a return is needed. This applies **even to opened food, treats and litter**.

3.2 **Report within 7 days.** Please report the problem within **7 days** of delivery by emailing **support@pawtella.com** with your order number and **clear photos** of the item, the damage or defect, and the packaging and shipping label. For recalls, contact us as soon as you become aware.

3.3 **Resolution.** Depending on the situation and your preference, we will arrange a replacement, a refund, or (where you choose) store credit or Paw Points, and we will tell you whether the item needs to be returned or disposed of.

## 4. How to Start a Return

1. Email **support@pawtella.com** with your **order number** and the items you want to return, and (for damaged, defective, recalled or wrong items) **photos**.
2. We will confirm eligibility and send **return instructions**, including the return address or a prepaid label where we are covering return shipping.
3. Pack the items securely in their original packaging and include your order number.
4. Ship the return (or drop it off / arrange pickup as instructed). We recommend keeping your tracking number.

Please do not send items back without contacting us first; unauthorized returns may be delayed or refused.

## 5. Refunds

5.1 **Method.** Approved refunds are issued to your **original payment method**. If that is not possible, or if you prefer, we may offer **store credit or Paw Points** instead.

5.2 **Timing.** Once we **receive and inspect** your return, we will process the refund and notify you. Refunds are normally issued within **5–10 business days** after we receive the return. It may take additional time for your bank or card issuer to post the refund to your account.

5.3 **What we refund.** We refund the price you paid for the returned items and applicable taxes. **Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error** (for example a damaged, defective, recalled or wrong item). See Section 7 for return shipping costs.

## 6. Paw Points and Paw Point Packs

6.1 **Points spent are restored.** If you paid wholly or partly with **Paw Points** and your order is refunded, the **Paw Points you spent are restored** to your account. Points earned on a purchase that is later refunded, cancelled or charged back may be **reversed**, as described in the Terms.

6.2 **Paw Point packs.** Purchased **Paw Point packs** are **non-refundable once the points have been used**, except where the law requires a refund (for example for billing errors, unauthorized charges, or if we end the program without a reasonable opportunity to redeem). Unused, untouched packs may be refundable at our discretion or where the law requires.

## 7. Return Shipping Costs

7.1 **Our error.** If the return is due to our error — a damaged, defective, recalled or wrong item — **we pay return shipping**, and we will provide a prepaid label or reimburse reasonable return shipping costs.

7.2 **Change of mind.** For other eligible returns (for example you changed your mind about an unopened item), **you pay the return shipping cost**, and original shipping charges are not refunded. For pickup or local delivery orders, you can return the item to the Toronto Depot at no shipping cost (Section 8).

## 8. Pickup and Local Delivery Returns

For orders you picked up or received by local delivery, you may return eligible items in person at the **Toronto Depot, 891 College St, Toronto, ON M6H 1A1**, during posted hours, with your order number and proof of purchase. The same eligibility rules (Sections 1–3) apply.

## 9. Exchanges

We do not always offer direct exchanges. The fastest way to get a different item is usually to return the eligible item for a refund and place a new order. For damaged, defective, recalled or wrong items, we will arrange a replacement at no cost to you (Section 3).

## 10. Your Consumer Rights

**Nothing in this Policy limits or takes away your rights under applicable consumer protection law**, including the Ontario *Consumer Protection Act, 2002*, the Quebec *Consumer Protection Act*, and other provincial, territorial or US state laws. If you are a **Quebec consumer**, your rights under the *Consumer Protection Act* and the *Civil Code of Québec* — including any legal warranty of quality and any rights regarding non-conforming or defective goods — apply in full, and this Policy applies only to the extent it is consistent with them. Where a provision of this Policy conflicts with a right you cannot waive, that right prevails.

Manufacturer warranties, where offered, are provided by the manufacturer. As described in the Terms, Pawtella is a retailer and generally gives no warranty of its own, but this does not affect any non-excludable statutory warranties or conditions.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new Effective Date. The policy in effect when you placed your order applies to that order.
