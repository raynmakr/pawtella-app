**Effective Date:** [Effective Date] · **Version:** [Version 1.0]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Pawtella legal entity name]**, doing business as **Pawtella** (**"Pawtella," "we," "us"** or **"our"**), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you use https://pawtella.com, our apps, online store, Toronto Depot, social feed, AI features, Paw Points and Subscribe & Save (the **"Services"**). We comply with Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (**PIPEDA**), and, where they apply, Quebec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (as amended by **Law 25**) and US state privacy laws.

> **The short version.** We collect what we need to run your account, your orders, the social feed and our AI pet assistant. **We do not sell your personal information.** Pet health information you share with the AI assistant is saved to memory **only with your express consent**, and you can **view, edit, delete or turn off AI Memory** at any time. **We never store payment card data or passwords in AI Memory.** We strip location (GPS) metadata from photos you upload. Some of our service providers store and process data **in the United States**. You can access, correct or delete your information by contacting our Privacy Officer at [privacy@pawtella.com].

## Key Points at a Glance

This summary is for convenience only. The full Policy below controls.

- **What we collect:** account details, pet profiles, posts and media, orders (through Shopify), city-level location, and device and usage data.
- **AI:** the AI assistant uses third-party AI providers. AI Memory is yours to control, and health details are saved only with your consent.

We collect personal information **directly from you**, **automatically** when you use the Services, and **from third parties** such as Shopify and payment processors.

**2.6 Device and usage data.** IP address, device type, operating system, browser, app version, language, referring pages, pages and features used, search terms, clicks, the date and time of visits, crash logs and performance data. **2.7 AI chat and AI Memory.** Your conversations with the AI assistant, your prompts and uploads to AI image and video tools, the AI Output, and your AI Memory (Section 3).

**2.8 Communications.** Messages you send to customer support, survey responses, and your marketing preferences and consent records.

**2.9 Information about other people.** If you mention other people, such as your veterinarian, groomer or a household member, you confirm you are entitled to share that information. Please limit it to what is needed, such as a business name and contact details.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information about **you** (such as your own health, religion or financial account details) and ask that you not share it in chat or posts.

## 3. The AI Assistant and AI Memory

**3.1 How the AI assistant works.** When you use the AI assistant or the AI image and video tools, we send your prompt, relevant conversation history, relevant AI Memory, and relevant product catalogue information to a **third-party AI provider**. The provider processes them to generate a response (Section 6.2). The AI assistant gives **general information only and is not veterinary advice** (see Section 5 of the Terms).

**3.2 What AI Memory is.** AI Memory is a profile linked to your account that helps the assistant personalize answers over time. It may include:

- **Owner details:** your first name, household (for example "two cats, one dog"), preferences, budget and favourite brands;
- **Pet details:** names, species, breeds, ages, weights, diets and behaviours;
- **Pet health questions and information:** for example allergies, sensitivities, conditions, medications and symptoms you asked about;
- **Vendors:** veterinarians, groomers, trainers, walkers or other providers you mention, and related notes such as appointment reminders you ask us to remember.

**3.3 Pet health information requires your express consent.** We treat pet health information as **sensitive**, because it can reveal information about you and your household. **We will save pet health information to AI Memory only if you give express consent**, through a separate opt-in in the app. You can use the AI assistant without giving this consent. You can **withdraw consent at any time** in settings. When you do, we stop saving new health information and delete the health information already stored in memory.

**3.4 What we never store in AI Memory.** **We never intentionally store payment card numbers, bank details, passwords, one-time passcodes or government identification numbers in AI Memory.** We use automated filters to keep them out. If you type them into chat anyway, we take steps to exclude them from memory and to limit how long they are kept in logs. Please don't share them.

**3.5 Your controls.** In **Account → AI Memory** you can:

- **view** everything stored in your AI Memory;
- **edit** or correct any item;
- **delete** individual items or **delete all** of your AI Memory;
- **turn AI Memory off** completely. When it is off, the assistant won't save or use memories, but it can still answer questions within a single conversation.

Deletions take effect in active systems promptly and are removed from backups within [35 days].

**3.6 Account deletion purges AI Memory.** **When you delete your account, your AI Memory, including all pet health information, is permanently deleted** from active systems within [30 days] and from backups within [35 days] after that.

**3.7 No model training by our providers.** We use AI providers under business terms that **do not permit them to use your inputs or outputs to train their AI models**. Providers may keep data for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Section 6.2. We may use **de-identified** or aggregated chat data to improve Pawtella's own features and safety systems.

**3.8 Automated processing.** The AI assistant generates suggestions automatically, and we use automated tools to help detect spam, fraud and content that breaks our rules. **We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.** If an automated tool restricts your content or account, you can ask for a human review by contacting us. Quebec residents have the right to be informed of decisions based exclusively on automated processing, to learn the personal information and main factors used, and to have that information corrected.

## 5. Photos, Videos and Media Processing

We also remove embedded metadata from videos where technically possible. Please remember that the **content** of a photo or video (for example a street sign, house number or a person's face) can still reveal information, so check what you share. We may create resized copies, thumbnails and transcoded versions of media to display it efficiently, and we may use automated tools to detect content that violates our rules.
