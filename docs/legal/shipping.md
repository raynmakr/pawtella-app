# Pawtella Shipping Policy

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026 · **Version:** 1.0

This Shipping Policy explains how **Bone & Biscuit College West**, doing business as **Pawtella** (**"Pawtella," "we," "us"** or **"our"**), prepares, ships, delivers and makes available for pickup the orders you place at https://pawtella.com and through our Services. It forms part of our Terms of Use (https://pawtella.com/terms) and should be read with our Returns & Refunds Policy (https://pawtella.com/returns). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms.

> **The short version.** We ship across **Canada and the United States**, with **rates and delivery options shown at checkout** before you pay. You can also choose **free pickup** at our Toronto Depot or, if you are **within about 15 km** of the depot, **local delivery**. Subscribe & Save orders follow these same rules. Delivery dates are estimates. If something arrives late, lost or damaged, contact us at support@pawtella.com and we will make it right.

## Key Points at a Glance

This summary is for convenience only. The full Policy below controls.

- **Where we ship:** addresses in Canada and the United States.
- **Pickup:** free, at the Toronto Depot, 891 College St.
- **Local delivery:** within about 15 km of the depot.
- **Rates and times:** calculated and shown at checkout, based on your address, the items and the option you choose.
- **US orders:** may be subject to duties, taxes and customs, and to restrictions on some pet foods and animal products.
- **Subscribe & Save:** recurring deliveries follow the same shipping rules.

---

## 1. Fulfillment Options

When you check out, you can choose from the options available for your address:

- **Shipping** to a Canadian or US address;
- **Free pickup** at our Toronto Depot; or
- **Local delivery**, if your address is within our local delivery area.

Not every option is available for every order or address. Paw Point packs are digital and do not require shipping, pickup or delivery.

## 2. Where We Ship

2.1 **Service area.** We ship to addresses in **Canada and the United States** only. We do not currently ship to other countries, to freight forwarders, or (for some items) to P.O. boxes or parcel lockers. Some products, promotions, pickup and local delivery may not be available in all areas.

2.2 **Restricted and regulated items.** Certain pet foods, treats, supplements and animal products are regulated and may not be shippable to every destination, especially across the Canada–US border. If an item cannot be shipped to your address, we will tell you and cancel or adjust that part of the order and refund it.

## 3. Shipping Rates and Delivery Times

3.1 **Rates.** Shipping rates are **calculated at checkout** based on your delivery address, the size and weight of your items, and the shipping option you select. The exact amount is shown to you **before you pay**. Taxes are added as required by law.

3.2 **Delivery times.** Any delivery estimate shown at checkout or in your confirmation is an **estimate only**, measured in business days after an order ships (not after you place it), and does not include our order-processing time (Section 6). Estimates are not guaranteed except where we specifically commit to a date. Carrier delays, weather, customs, remote destinations and peak periods can affect delivery.

3.3 **Carriers.** We ship through third-party carriers and delivery partners. Once an order is handed to a carrier, delivery is subject to that carrier's handling and schedule.

## 4. Free Pickup at the Toronto Depot

4.1 **How it works.** Choose **"Pickup"** at checkout. Pickup is **free**. We will notify you when your order is **ready for pickup**. Please wait for that notice before coming in.

4.2 **Where.** Pick up your order at the **Toronto Depot, 891 College St, Toronto, ON M6H 1A1, Canada** during posted hours. Bring your **order number**, and photo ID if we ask for it. If someone else is picking up for you, they will need your order number and we may ask for authorization.

4.3 **Hold period.** Please pick up your order promptly. Orders not picked up within the hold period shown in your ready-for-pickup notice may be cancelled and refunded, less any non-refundable charges disclosed at checkout.

## 5. Local Delivery

5.1 **Where.** Local delivery is available to addresses **within approximately 15 km of our Toronto Depot** (891 College St), as determined by our delivery tools. Eligibility is confirmed at checkout based on your address.

5.2 **Fees and timing.** Any local delivery fee and the available delivery windows are **shown at checkout**. Delivery timing is an estimate.

5.3 **Access and receipt.** Please provide an accurate address and any access instructions (buzzer code, unit number, gate). If you are not home, we may leave the order in a safe place where you have authorized us to do so, or arrange redelivery. Redelivery may incur an additional fee shown at the time.

## 6. Order Processing

6.1 **Processing time.** We need time to pick, pack and prepare your order before it ships or is ready for pickup or delivery. Processing time is in addition to any shipping estimate.

6.2 **Confirmation vs. acceptance.** An order confirmation email means we **received** your order; it is not acceptance. As described in the Terms, we accept your order when we ship it or make it available for pickup or delivery, and we may refuse, limit or cancel an order for lawful reasons (including stock issues, suspected fraud, or pricing or description errors).

6.3 **Out of stock.** If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we will tell you and, where practical, let you choose to wait, substitute a comparable item **only with your consent**, or cancel that item for a refund. We will not substitute pet food or litter without your consent.

## 7. US Orders: Duties, Taxes and Customs

7.1 **Duties and import taxes.** Orders shipped to the United States may be subject to **import duties, taxes, brokerage and customs processing fees**. Unless clearly stated otherwise at checkout, you are responsible for these as the importer of record, and they are **not** included in the product price or shipping charge.

7.2 **Customs and restrictions.** Cross-border shipments may be inspected and delayed by customs, and some pet foods and animal products are **restricted or prohibited** from import. If a shipment is refused or seized because of import restrictions, or returned to us, we will work with you on a refund in line with our Returns & Refunds Policy, less any non-recoverable shipping or customs costs.

## 8. Subscribe & Save Deliveries

8.1 **Same shipping rules.** Recurring **Subscribe & Save** orders follow this Shipping Policy, including service area, rates shown at checkout, pickup and local delivery eligibility, and US duties and customs. Shipping charges and taxes for each recurring order are calculated when that order is processed.

8.2 **Out-of-stock cycles.** As described in the Terms, if a subscribed item is out of stock when a delivery is due, we will skip that item for that cycle and you will not be charged for it; the subscription resumes on the next scheduled cycle.

## 9. Tracking, Delivery and Risk of Loss

9.1 **Tracking.** Where tracking is available, we will provide it after your order ships. Tracking is provided by the carrier.

9.2 **Risk of loss and title.** To the extent permitted by law, title and **risk of loss pass to you when the order is delivered** to the address you provide (including when it is left there in line with your instructions or the carrier's practice), or when it is handed to you or your designate at pickup.

## 10. Late, Lost, Damaged or Stolen Packages

10.1 **Late or lost in transit.** If a package is significantly late or appears lost **in transit before delivery**, contact us at **support@pawtella.com** within **7 days** of the estimated delivery date and we will work with the carrier to locate it, replace it or refund you.

10.2 **Damaged in transit.** If your order arrives damaged, keep the packaging and contact us within **7 days** of delivery with **photos**. See our Returns & Refunds Policy for how we replace or refund damaged items.

10.3 **Stolen after delivery.** To the extent permitted by law, we are **not responsible for packages stolen after delivery** to the address you provided. We will still try to help you with the carrier where we can.

## 11. Address Accuracy

You are responsible for providing a **complete and accurate** shipping or delivery address and contact details. We are not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable because of an address error, we will contact you to arrange reshipment (additional shipping may apply) or a refund of the product price, less non-recoverable shipping costs.

## 12. Your Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Policy limits your rights under applicable consumer protection law, including the Ontario *Consumer Protection Act, 2002*, the Quebec *Consumer Protection Act*, and other provincial, territorial or US state laws. For example, where the law gives you the right to cancel for non-delivery within a set time, this Policy does not take that right away.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new Effective Date. Changes do not apply to orders we have already accepted.
